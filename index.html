About

Parker Difuntorum earned an M.S. in Electrical Engineering from the University of Florida and a B.S. in Electrical Engineering from California State University, Sacramento. He is a power systems engineer, researcher, inventor, and U.S. Coast Guard Veteran with experience across utility control center operations, distribution planning, SCADA/EMS, RF communications, high-performance computing, AI/IoT research, cloud-connected sensing, and cyber-physical infrastructure resilience. At the University of Florida, he conducted graduate research in AI, IoT, embedded systems, hardware security, and cyber-physical systems and contributed to peer-reviewed publications and patented inventions assigned to the University of Florida Research Foundation.

Research Focus

Research interests include cyber-physical grid resilience, public-data observability, communications-aware state estimation, digital twins, edge sensing, sensor fusion, embedded AI, and practical automation for operational infrastructure.
